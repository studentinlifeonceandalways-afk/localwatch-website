Common Issues

The parser is not capturing data (shows "Pending" or "Error"):
Google occasionally changes their page layout. Try clicking the "Retry" button. If the issue persists, please email us and we will push an update to fix the parser.

I'm not receiving notifications:
Ensure that Chrome and your operating system (Windows/Mac) have notifications enabled for Chrome. You can check this in Chrome Settings > Privacy and Security > Site Settings > Notifications.
